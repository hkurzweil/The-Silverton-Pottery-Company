The Silverton Pottery Company

Mugs, bowls, cups and pots — every single one thrown and fired right here in town, in the scrappy yellow building down from the train tracks.

↑ you'll know it by the flags & the hand-painted sign

Pottery, fabric art, patches and hats. Thursday through Sunday, 11 to 4, all summer season.

Hand thrown, hand glazed

No two are ever the same

Every piece is thrown one at a time on the wheel and glazed by hand. The glaze breaks and runs where it wants to in the kiln, so the one you pick up is the only one of it that exists.

Start to finish, in town

9,318feet above sea level

Wedged, thrown, trimmed, glazed and fired in the same little building you can walk into. Nothing gets shipped in from somewhere else and stickered — if it's on the shelf, it came off the wheel about thirty feet from where you're standing.

Alongside the pots you'll find fabric art, patches and hats, all made by hand too.
